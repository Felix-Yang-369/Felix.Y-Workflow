# 写实学科封面提示词

## 新生成

按实际主题替换方括号内容, 不让模板中的场景压过用户指定素材

```text
Create one finished 16:9 cinematic photographic cover for [EXACT DISCIPLINE]. Authentic [SCENE], physically credible [HERO], real material textures and spatial perspective. Graphite-dark detail with natural side lighting and atmospheric depth, restrained [ACCENT] highlights and actual practical lighting. Keep the subject recognizable on the center-right and a dark readable title area in the upper-left. Photograph-led, not an isometric miniature, diagram collage or fantasy scene.
Set the exact uppercase title on [ONE OR TWO] lines in the upper-left: "[LINE 1]" and "[LINE 2]". Large bold condensed sans-serif, metallic warm white first line, restrained [ACCENT] second line, left aligned with generous margins. Spell every letter correctly. No extra words, labels, slogans, numbers, formulas, logos or watermark. Preserve realistic colors and avoid excessive neon. Exactly 16:9 landscape.
```

## 基于用户照片

```text
Use the supplied photograph as the primary source and recompose it into a finished 16:9 cinematic cover for [DISCIPLINE]. Preserve its recognizable [SUBJECTS], real structural relationships, materials, perspective and light direction. Widen or reframe gracefully without replacing the photograph with a different scene. Refine contrast and [ACCENT] color grading while retaining natural light and credible surface detail. Add the exact uppercase title "[TITLE]" in the upper-left, [LINE BREAKS], bold condensed sans-serif, warm-white first line and [ACCENT] second line. Darken behind the title only as needed for clear reading. No invented machines, diagram panels, extra words, slogans or watermark.
```

## 本系列已验证的主题选择

| 学科 | 主体与环境 | 强调色 | 避免主题偏移 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| INDUSTRIAL ENGINEERING | 真实大型厂房、钢结构、工作平台、桥式吊车, 保留纵深及生产系统语境 | 橙色 | 不替换成等距玩具工厂; 厂房仅代表工业工程的一类应用 |
| CHEMICAL ENGINEERING | 不锈钢反应器、分离设备、换热器、阀门与工艺管线, 工艺装置空间 | 淡紫色 | 不以烧杯或分子图代替过程工程; 不制造发光紫色液体 |
| MATERIALS SCIENCE | 金属、陶瓷、复合材料与聚合物样品, 材料研究工作台, 背景拉伸测试设备 | 紫色 | 不使用巨型矿晶、浮空原子或化工反应器; 微观组织放大需要标明为示意, 不能伪装成裸眼尺度照片 |

参考成品体现摄影风格、主体选择和排版, 不证明其中每个设备细节都可作为科学教学图。新的材料科学封面优先以真实金相试样外观为准, 如需微观组织可在背景显微镜屏幕中表现, 不把显微晶粒放大后称为真实宏观截面

## 保留主体的标题修正

```text
Edit only the title and its immediate background. Set the exact text "[TITLE]", uppercase, [LINE BREAKS], upper-left, large bold condensed sans-serif. Keep the main subject, scene, lighting, palette, perspective and 16:9 composition unchanged. Add no other words. Improve local contrast only as needed. Spell every letter correctly.
```
